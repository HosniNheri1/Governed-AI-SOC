# Risk Register — Governed AI-Assisted SOC (lab scope)

**Status:** draft for review. Ratings are proposals, revised on 2026-11-11 against the Day 5 results.
**Method:** NIST SP 800-30 style qualitative assessment (likelihood × impact: Very Low / Low / Moderate / High / Very High), extended from an earlier NIST SP 800-30 register by the same author.
**Scope:** lab (attacker VM, victim containers, Suricata sensors, Splunk, ML scoring service) plus the Shuffle workflow, which runs in the Shuffle cloud outside the lab boundary. Implemented on a defined scope, not certified.
**Standards:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A (controls selected for what the lab exercises), ISO/IEC 42001 (AI-specific risks).
**Owner of every risk:** Hosni Nheri (sole contributor).
